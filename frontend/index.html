How it works

This project measures structural properties of a passage — sentence rhythm, vocabulary variety, punctuation habits, and readability — and weighs them against patterns typical of human and AI writing. The result is an estimate, read alongside the underlying measurements, not a verdict.

About the detector

No detector, including this one, can identify authorship with certainty. Short passages, heavily edited AI text, and unusual human writing styles can all produce false positives or false negatives. Use the result as one signal among several.
